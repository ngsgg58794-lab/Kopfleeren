What does Croesus do?

Croesus is a satirical joke app: a certificate, a wealth ticker, champagne, a butler and a minimum-wage calculator. It has no practical use, and that is the joke.

Where is my data?

Only on your device. There is no account and no cloud. Details in the privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes all data.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. To request a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.
