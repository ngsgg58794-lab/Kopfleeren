In short

Croesus does not collect personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics or tracking services, no third-party SDKs and no server operated by the developer.

Local storage

The name you enter, the income figure you set and the date of first launch are stored only locally on your device. They are not transmitted to the developer or any third party. Depending on your device settings they may be included in your device backup (iCloud), which is governed by Apple's terms.

Sharing

The certificate is shared only when you tap “Show off”, using the system share sheet. You choose where they go.

App Store

When you purchase and download the app, Apple processes data under its own responsibility. The developer has no influence on this and receives no payment data.

Contact by email

If you email me, I process your email address and message only to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR) and delete them once they are no longer needed.
