## EN
**Name:** Croesus - Satire App
**Subtitle:** A joke app about being rich
**Promotional Text:** A joke app with no practical use. Pop champagne, call your butler, watch a ticker count imaginary income. It costs €100, and that is the joke.
**Description:**
Croesus is a joke app with no practical use. It costs €100, and that is the joke.

WHAT YOU GET
• Certificate: a gold certificate with your name that says, truthfully, that you paid €100 for an app that does nothing. Shareable as an image.
• Wealth Ticker: enter any annual income and watch an imaginary counter tick up
• Champagne: pop the cork, with haptic feedback
• Butler: 40+ lines depending on the time of day, plus orders you can give him
• Peasant Calculator: how many minimum-wage hours does something cost?

Everything in Croesus is fiction and proves nothing about anyone's wealth. It does not create documents, calls or messages that imitate real ones.

Available in English and German. The app collects no personal data and has no ads, accounts or in-app purchases.
**Keywords:** satire,joke,luxury,butler,champagne,wealth,millionaire,gag,humor,money,funny
**What's New:** First release.
**Review Notes:** Satirical novelty app. Features: certificate (states the user paid €100 for a joke app), wealth ticker with an imaginary counter, haptic champagne animation, butler with text lines, minimum-wage calculator. The price is intentional and part of the joke. After the previous rejection (1.1.6) we removed the boarding-pass generator and the fictional call screens, which could be used to imitate real things. No login, no data collection, no purchases.

## Antwort an Apple (Resolution Center)
Hello,

thank you for the review.

Guideline 3 – Price: Yes, $99.99 is the intended price. Croesus is a satirical novelty app, and the price is part of the joke: the in-app certificate states plainly that the user paid €100 for an app that does nothing.

Guideline 1.1.6: We removed the features that could be used to imitate real things or deceive others: the fictional boarding-pass generator and the fictional incoming-call screens. The remaining features (certificate, wealth ticker with an imaginary counter, champagne animation, butler text lines, minimum-wage calculator) do not create fake documents, calls or system behavior, and nothing in the app or its metadata claims real wealth or membership. We also changed the app name, subtitle, description and screenshots accordingly. A new build with these changes is attached to this submission.

Thank you.
