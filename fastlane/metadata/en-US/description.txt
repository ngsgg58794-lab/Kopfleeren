Too much on your mind? Calmdrop helps you let go of your thoughts – in seconds.

Just say or type everything that's on your mind: appointments, groceries, calls, paperwork, grandma's birthday card. One thought per line. Calmdrop automatically sorts the rest into clear groups – so you can focus on what matters again.

HOW IT WORKS
• Drop: type or tap the microphone and just speak freely
• Sort: Calmdrop files every thought automatically – Appointments, Family & kids, Household, Work, Paperwork & admin
• Check off: tick off what's done with a single tap and get it out of sight

WHY CALMDROP
• Fast: from thought to list in just a few seconds
• Voice input: perfect on the go or when your hands are full
• Calm ocean-blue design – light and dark mode
• English and German, switch anytime in the app
• Private: your entries stay on your device. No account, no sign-up.

For everyone who knows the mental load: parents, professionals, students – and anyone with too much on their mind at night.

Drop it. Breathe. Find your inner calm.
